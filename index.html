About Me

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Cross-Disciplinary Physics and Complex Systems (IFISC) and the University of the Balearic Islands.

My research lies at the intersection of dynamical systems and network science. During my PhD I focused on graph dynamical systems, where the evolving object is a network rather than a point in phase space. In particular, I investigate how concepts from dynamical systems theory—such as sensitivity to initial conditions and Lyapunov exponents— can be extended to temporal networks.

Research Interests

Temporal Networks

Dynamics, predictability and information flow in systems with time-varying interactions.

Dynamical Systems

Chaos, Lyapunov exponents, sensitivity to initial conditions, and nonlinear dynamics.

Stochastic Processes

Mathematical modelling of systems subject to randomness, fluctuations and uncertainty.

Complex Systems

Understanding collective behaviour in ecological, social, biological and technological systems.

Current Research

My recent work investigates the characterization of temporal network dynamics when node identities are unavailable, the predictability of evolving networks, and the impact of environmental variability on ecological systems.
