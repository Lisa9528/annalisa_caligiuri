About me

I am a researcher in complex systems, network science, and dynamical systems. My work focuses on understanding how the dynamics of complex networks can be characterized, predicted, and affected by structural and environmental changes.

Research interests

  • Complex networks
  • Temporal networks
  • Dynamical systems
  • Ecological and brain network dynamics
  • Predictability and stability of complex systems

Projects

My recent work investigates how network dynamics can be analyzed when labels are lost, and how environmental variability affects interaction networks.
