About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Cross-Disciplinary Physics and Complex Systems (IFISC) and the University of the Balearic Islands.

My research lies at the intersection of dynamical systems and network science. I am interested in graph dynamical systems, temporal networks, predictability, sensitivity to initial conditions, and the role of internal and external perturbations in complex systems.

Research

My work focuses on systems where interactions evolve in time. I study how dynamical-systems concepts can be extended from trajectories in phase space to trajectories of networks.

Temporal Networks

Dynamics and predictability in time-varying interactions.

Dynamical Systems

Chaos, Lyapunov exponents, sensitivity and nonlinear dynamics.

Stochastic Processes

Stochastic differential equations, birth–death processes, Markov processes, and stochastic models of interacting systems.

Complex Systems

Collective behaviour in ecological, biological and social systems.

Unlabelled Temporal Networks

Brain Network Predictability

Can temporal-network methods help characterize ageing and brain pathology? We investigate how predictable brain-network dynamics change across different conditions.

Opinion Dynamics in Changing Environments

How does collective opinion formation change when the environment itself evolves in time? We show that time-varying influences can dramatically affect consensus formation and fluctuations.
