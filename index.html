About me

I am a researcher in physics of complex systems, with a background in network science and dynamical systems. My work focuses on understanding how the dynamics of complex networks can be characterized, predicted, and affected by structural and environmental changes.

Research interests

Complex networks

Modeling and analyzing interacting systems through graph-based representations.

Temporal networks

Studying systems whose interactions change over time.

Dynamical systems

Understanding predictability, stability, and sensitivity to perturbations.

Ecological and brain networks

Applying network-based methods to empirical systems and real-world data.

Current work

My recent work investigates what information about network dynamics can be recovered when node labels are lost, and how environmental variability may reshape interaction patterns in complex systems.
